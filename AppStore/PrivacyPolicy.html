NotchGate Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

NotchGate is made by NotchLens. It runs on your Mac. We do not run an account system and we do not sell personal data.

What the app uses

  • Calendar — next events, only if you turn Calendar on and grant access (Pro).
  • Location — local weather, only if you turn Weather on and grant access (Pro).
  • Local apps — only the applications you explicitly pin to app slots.
  • Music controls — if you connect Apple Music or Spotify, NotchGate reads the current track and sends only playback commands you request. Track information stays on your Mac.
  • File shelf — NotchGate stores local security-scoped references and filenames for files you explicitly add. The shelf is a Pro feature; removing a shelf item is always available and never deletes the original file.
  • AirDrop — a Pro feature available only when you explicitly choose to share a shelf item with Apple’s AirDrop service and select recipients.
  • App Store — purchase and restore of NotchGate Pro. Receipt checks go to Apple, not to NotchLens servers.

What stays on your Mac

Settings, pinned apps, Pomodoro position, search history (if you keep it), and license state in Keychain stay on this Mac.

Limited third-party processing

If you enable Weather, NotchGate sends the coordinates returned by macOS Location Services to Open-Meteo to retrieve a forecast. NotchLens does not receive or store those coordinates, and NotchGate does not send calendar events, pinned-app data, or search history to NotchLens or Open-Meteo. macOS and Apple may process location data as described in Apple’s system privacy settings.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising. We do not capture your screen, require an account, or send music metadata or file contents to NotchLens.

Retention and consent

On-device settings and search history remain until you remove them in the app or uninstall it. Calendar and location access can be revoked at any time in macOS System Settings; the app stops using each source when access is revoked. Weather requests are transient and are not retained by NotchLens. To request help deleting data or to ask a privacy question, contact support using the support URL listed on the App Store product page.
