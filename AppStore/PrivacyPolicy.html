NotchGate Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

NotchGate is made by NotchLens. It runs on your Mac. We do not run an account system and we do not sell personal data.

What the app uses

  • Calendar — next events, only if you turn Calendar on and grant access (Pro).
  • Location — local weather, only if you turn Weather on and grant access (Pro). Approximate coordinates rounded to about 1 km are sent directly to Open-Meteo for the forecast; NotchLens does not receive them.
  • Apple Events — Music and Spotify, to show Now Playing.
  • Local applications — apps you pin to NotchGate.
  • App Store — purchase and restore of NotchGate Pro. Receipt checks go to Apple, not to NotchLens servers.

What stays on your Mac

Settings, pinned apps, Pomodoro position, search history (if you keep it), and license state in Keychain stay on this Mac.

What we do not do

We do not capture your screen or record screen content. We do not sell your information. We do not use your calendar or location for advertising. We do not require an account.
